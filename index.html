Thesis

The Aperture Theory of Awareness proposes that experience is not generated by time or consciousness, but structured through constraint.

All moments exist within a unified spacetime structure, yet experience appears sequential, personal, and bounded because access to this structure is locally constrained. Biological organisms function as apertures through which awareness becomes temporally and perspectivally localised.

Time, identity, and continuity are not features of reality itself, but of how experience is organised under constraint.

Experience = Access to Block × Constraint

About ATA

ATA explores the relationship between awareness, experience, biological localisation, constraint and the structure of spacetime.

The framework distinguishes between what exists and what can become accessible as experience from a particular perspective.
